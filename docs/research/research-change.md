Date of research: 2026-10-02. Not legal advice. Confidence tags: [H] primary text read or multiple consistent sources; [M] one secondary source or my own legal background knowledge not re-verified this session; [L] weak, vendor, or unverified.

Tool note: several primary sources (Legifrance, BOE, travail-industrie.com, irishstatutebook, GDPRhub, APQC) returned 403/404 during this session. Where that happened, the claim rests on a secondary source and is tagged accordingly. Counsel in each country must confirm.

## 1. Germany (BetrVG)

**Statutory text (read directly)**
- BetrVG s.87(1) opening words: works council co-determines "soweit eine gesetzliche oder tarifliche Regelung nicht besteht". Nr. 5: "Aufstellung allgemeiner Urlaubsgrundsaetze und des Urlaubsplans sowie die Festsetzung der zeitlichen Lage des Urlaubs fuer einzelne Arbeitnehmer, wenn zwischen dem Arbeitgeber und den beteiligten Arbeitnehmern kein Einverstaendnis erzielt wird". Nr. 6: "Einfuehrung und Anwendung von technischen Einrichtungen, die dazu bestimmt sind, das Verhalten oder die Leistung der Arbeitnehmer zu ueberwachen". s.87(2): if no agreement, the Einigungsstelle (conciliation board) decides and its award replaces the agreement. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/betrvg/__87.html [H]
- s.90(1) Nr. 3 (current text): employer must inform the works council in good time of planned work procedures and workflows "einschliesslich des Einsatzes von Kuenstlicher Intelligenz", with documents. s.90(2): employer must consult on planned measures and their effects early enough that the council's proposals can influence planning. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/betrvg/__90.html [H]
- s.80(2): employer must inform the council "rechtzeitig und umfassend" so it can perform its tasks; documents on request. s.80(3): council may call in an expert by agreement; expert deemed necessary for AI. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/betrvg/__80.html [H]

**Does a new absence/HR system need co-determination?**
- Nr. 6 test: a system is "bestimmt" to monitor if it is objectively suitable to collect and record behaviour or performance data; the employer's intent is irrelevant. Shown for Microsoft Office 365 in BAG 1 ABR 20/21 (8 Mar 2022). https://www.humanresourcesmanager.de/mitbestimmung-bei-softwareeinfuehrung-worauf-kommt-es-an/ [M, secondary]; also https://www.anwaltonline.com/arbeitsrecht/urteile/31511/technische-ueberwachungseinrichtung-mitbestimmung-des-gesamtbetriebsrats [M]
- Absence/sickness data per named employee (dates, frequency, Krankenrueckkehrgespraeche, Bradford-style reports) is behaviour data, so Nr. 6 is very likely triggered. I found no case specifically on an absence module, so this is inference from the objective-suitability test. [M]
- Nr. 5 is narrower. It covers general leave principles, the leave plan, and timing for individual employees if there is no agreement. A tool that only implements an existing policy may not trigger Nr. 5, but changing approval rules, blackout periods or carry-over rules in the workflow would. [M, my reading of the text]
- Competence: BAG 1 ABR 45/11 (25 Sep 2012) held the Konzernbetriebsrat is competent for group-wide personnel-administration software processing behaviour/performance data across group companies (s.58(1) with s.87(1) Nr. 6). https://betriebs-berater.ruw.de/arbeitsrecht/urteile/Konzernbetriebsrat-ist-zustaendig-fuer-Personalverwaltungssysteme-14435 [M]. Gesamtbetriebsrat is competent where a company-wide system technically requires uniform rules. https://www.weka.de/betriebsrat-personalrat/bag-bestaetigt-gesamtbetriebsrat-bestimmt-ueber-softwareeinfuehrung/ [M]. Implication: a Groupon DE entity-level or Berlin-site council may not be the only counterpart; the right body depends on how many German entities and councils exist. I did not verify Groupon's German council structure. [L]

**Consequence if skipped**
- Works council has an Unterlassungsanspruch (claim to stop) where the employer breaches s.87 co-determination; a slight breach plus repetition risk suffices, and the employer's mere assurance of future compliance is not enough. Interim relief (einstweilige Verfuegung) is available in principle. https://www.betriebsrat.de/betriebsratslexikon/br/unterlassungsanspruch-betriebsverfassung [M, union/publisher site]; https://www.humanresourcesmanager.de/mitbestimmung-bei-softwareeinfuehrung-worauf-kommt-es-an/ [M]
- Doctrinal anchor (from my background knowledge, not re-verified): BAG recognises the general s.23(3) analogue/Unterlassungsanspruch under s.87 (BAG 3 May 1994, 1 ABR 24/93). [M]
- Practice is mixed. LAG Duesseldorf 9 Jan 2018, 3 TaBVGa 6/17 rejected the council's interim injunction applications on a software matter. https://www.haufe.de/id/entscheidung/lag-duesseldorf-beschluss-vom-09012018-3-tabvga-617-HI11635991.html [M, snippet only]. Courts have also refused injunctions that would block planned changes until information/consultation is complete (s.90 alone is weaker than s.87). [M]
- "Theorie der Wirksamkeitsvoraussetzung" (measures against employees void without consent) is the usual extra risk for individual measures, for example warnings or leave decisions made with a non-agreed tool. I did not verify its application to system introduction. [L]
- Practical consequence for the plan: assume the German go-live needs a Betriebsvereinbarung (or Einigungsstelle award) and budget 3-9 months for negotiation. The 3-9 month figure is my planning assumption, not sourced. [L]

## 3. Ireland and UK

- UK: Information and Consultation of Employees Regulations 2004 apply to undertakings with 50+ employees, triggered by a valid request from 10% of employees (minimum 15, maximum 2,500) or employer initiative. Once standing arrangements exist, reg. 20 requires information and consultation on decisions likely to lead to substantial changes in work organisation. https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/3426/regulation/20 [H on reg. 20]; threshold and request mechanics from https://www.lewissilkin.com/en/insights/information-and-consultation [M]. A simple absence tool is unlikely to meet "substantial changes" unless it alters working arrangements, and only if ICE arrangements exist (none found for Groupon UK). [L on Groupon facts]
- Ireland: Employees (Provision of Information and Consultation) Act 2006 applies to undertakings with 50+ employees; right is triggered by employee request (10% of employees) or negotiated arrangements. https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/2006/act/9/enacted/en/print (search snippet, fetch blocked) [M]; https://www.workplacerelations.ie/en/Publications_Forms/Guide_to_Information_and_Consultation_Act.pdf [not opened]. Same practical conclusion as UK. [M]
- Neither UK nor Ireland has a German-style co-determination right on monitoring software. Restructuring-driven collective consultation (redundancy) is separate and relevant to Project Foundry, not to this project. [M]

## 4. GDPR and data transfers

- Health data: sickness data revealing health status is Art. 9 special category data; employment-law basis is Art. 9(2)(b), with national rules (Germany: BDSG s.26(3)). I could not fetch s.26 BDSG this session. [M, background knowledge]
- DPIA (Art. 35): WP29/EDPB guidelines (WP248 rev.01) list criteria including sensitive data, large scale, vulnerable data subjects (employees, due to power imbalance), systematic monitoring and innovative technology; processing meeting two criteria generally requires a DPIA. https://www.scl.org/8959-gdpr-data-protection-impact-assessments/ [M, secondary summary of WP248]. A central cross-entity HR system holding sickness data on all employees meets at least three (sensitive, large scale, vulnerable subjects). Conclusion: treat a DPIA as required; additionally check each national supervisory authority list under Art. 35(4) (German DSK, CNIL, UODO, DPC, AEPD, UK ICO). Conservative reading. [M]
- Diagnosis vs incapacity, Germany: employer receives only an eAU data record from the health insurer (name, start/end of incapacity, date of medical finding, initial/follow-up flag, accident indicator), no diagnosis. Basis s.109 SGB IV with s.5 EFZG; in force since 1 Jan 2023; employee still has a duty to notify the employer; privately insured staff are outside the eAU retrieval. https://loschelder.de/fileadmin/redaktion/newsletter/pdf/arbeitsrecht/2023_01_17_2_eAU.pdf [M]; https://der-betrieb.de/meldungen/die-elektronische-arbeitsunfaehigkeitsbescheinigung/ [not opened]. Design implication: system should store only incapacity dates and type of absence code, never diagnosis fields or free-text. [M]
- Poland: employer retrieves e-ZLA through ZUS PUE without the disease code; the Labour Code art. 22(1) catalogue of employee data does not include health status. https://kadry.infor.pl/tematy/niezdolnosc-do-pracy/pracownik,18/ and https://kpmg.com/pl/pl/blogs/home/posts/2023/10/kontakt-z-pracownikiem-na-chorobowym-jak-to-wyglada-z-punktu-widzenia-rodo-i-prawa-pracy-blog-prawny.html [M]. Implication: Polish shared services staff handling absence cannot see diagnosis either; do not build a uniform "reason for absence" free-text field.
- EU-US transfer to US parent: EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Commission adequacy decision, 10 Jul 2023). General Court dismissed Latombe's challenge on 3 Sep 2025 (T-553/23); appeal lodged 31 Oct 2025 (C-703/25 P), pending before the Court of Justice. I found no judgment as of 2 Oct 2026. https://www.hunton.com/privacy-and-information-security-law/eus-general-court-confirms-adequacy-of-eu-u-s-data-privacy-framework ; https://www.wilmerhale.com/insights/blogs/wilmerhale-privacy-and-cybersecurity-law/20251201-european-court-of-justice-to-review-challenge-to-eu-us-data-privacy-framework ; https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/35459-latombe-filed-appeal-against-general-court-dismissal-of-challenge-to-european-unionunited-states-data-protection-framework-adequacy-decision-in-latombe-v-commission [M-H]. Whether Groupon, Inc. is self-certified under the DPF: not checked on dataprivacyframework.gov. [not verified]. Note DPF HR-data certification must be specifically elected. Recommend SCCs plus transfer impact assessment as fallback. [M]
- UK: EU renewed UK adequacy decisions to 27 Dec 2031. https://hunton.com/privacy-and-information-security-law/european-commission-renews-uk-data-adequacy-decisions ; https://www.lawsociety.ie/gazette/top-stories/2025/december/eu-renews-post-brexit-data-decision/ [M-H]. EU-to-UK flows therefore need no extra mechanism. UK-to-US transfers use the UK-US data bridge (not verified here).
- German works agreement also must cover data-protection design (BDSG s.26(4) allows collective agreements as legal basis). [M, background]

## 5. Benchmarks for absence/leave administration time and cost

Honest summary: I found no independent (APQC, CIPD, SHRM, Gartner, Deloitte, academic) source giving minutes per leave request or an HR time share spent on absence. All quantified per-request figures below are vendor-sponsored. Use as order-of-magnitude only.

- Vendor (Tilt, leave-management software; "2026 Leave Benchmark Report", methodology not disclosed on page): hours of HR effort per leave case by company size: <250 employees 3.9, 250-1,000 5.8, >1,000 6.3; manual 6.8, hybrid 4.2, automated/structured 2.6. https://hellotilt.com/blog/leave-managements-hidden-costs-what-hr-teams-are-actually-spending-time-on/ [L, vendor]. These are statutory leave cases (US FMLA-style long leaves), not routine annual-leave requests. Do not apply to simple vacation approvals.
- Vendor (Pulpstream, "State of Leave Management 2026 Benchmark Report"): assumes 40 cases/month x 7.5 hours x $30/hr = $57,600/yr HR labour; total $112,600/yr for a 500-employee firm, of which 60-75% claimed recoverable. Error remediation ($30,000) and opportunity cost ($25,000) are assumption-based. https://pulpstream.com/resources/blog/cost-of-manual-leave-administration-2026 [L, vendor]
- Payroll errors: claims of "APA 2025 benchmark 1.2% error rate (0.7-3.1%)" and "manual timecard errors cost 1-8% of gross payroll" appear on vendor/blog pages; I could not trace to an APA primary publication. https://timewellscheduled.com/news/time-and-attendance-statistics/ ; https://www.plansponsor.com/payroll-professionals-still-strive-for-accuracy [L]. Treat as unverified. Leave-specific error cost: not found.
- HR admin share of time: figures of 20-30% (attributed to BCG) and "up to 57%" (attributed to Deloitte-based analysis) appear only in aggregator pages without primary links. https://stealthagents.com/research/vp-of-hr-time-management-statistics-2026/ [L]. Gartner "fewer than 25% of HR hours are strategic" also second-hand. [L]. Absence-specific share: not found.
- CIPD (independent): HR-to-employee ratio median 1:60, interquartile 1:37 to 1:100, 5th-95th percentile 1:17 to 1:255 (survey of 1,174 UK HR decision makers, published 1 Mar 2023). Useful for sizing HR, not for absence time. https://www.cipd.org/en/views-and-insights/thought-leadership/insight/technology-data-hr-functions/ [H on figures]
- CIPD Health and Wellbeing at Work: average 6.6 days absence per employee per year (earlier edition: 804 organisations, 4.3m employees); check the 2025 edition for current figure. https://www.cipd.org/globalassets/media/knowledge/knowledge-hub/reports/2025-pdfs/8920-Health-and-wellbeing-report-2025- [M, figure from a search summary; not confirmed in 2025 edition]
- Recommended approach for the plan: present a range from a bottom-up model using Groupon's own request volumes and a time-and-motion sample (for example 5-15 minutes per routine request manually, versus under 2 minutes automated, as planning assumptions, not sourced) and label the vendor figures as upper-bound illustrations. [L]

## Key implications for the plan
1. Germany and the Netherlands (if present) are consent-based, so they gate the timeline; France, Spain are consultation-based; UK, Ireland, Italy are low-risk for this scope.
2. Run Foundry-related restructuring consultations and the absence-system consultations separately but sequenced, because both go to the same German/Polish bodies and councils may link them.
3. Design for data minimisation: no diagnosis fields, role-based access, DPIA before build, SCCs as DPF fallback while C-703/25 P is pending.
4. Benefits case must rely on internal baselines, not vendor benchmarks.
