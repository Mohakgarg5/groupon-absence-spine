## 2. Headcount (FY2025 10-K, same URL)
- 1,734 employees at 12/31/2025: North America 421 (254 sales, 167 corp/ops/support); International 1,313 (380 sales, 933 corp/ops/support). ~76% sit outside North America.
- Chicago HQ 377 employees (per https://sea.peoplemattersglobal.com/news/strategic-hr/groupon-layoffs-hit-hr-customer-service-and-tech-teams-amid-ai-restructuring-49965 - secondary; not verified in 10-K). 10-K refers to "600 West Chicago" as previously leased HQ.
- No per-country headcount found. Known hubs: Poland shared services (10-K "significant shared service operations"); Prague (Chief Product Officer Havlickova, CMO Buryan, recruiter roles: https://www.groupon.com/articles/team); job postings span Dublin, London, Madrid, Prague, Valencia, Warsaw (search snippet, unverified). India shared services entity exists.
- Restructuring: 2022 plan; Italy exit 2024. NEW: May 21, 2026 board approved "Project Foundry" cut of up to 400 positions (employees+contractors, ~23-25% of workforce), mostly by end Q3 2026; mandatory consultation may delay in some jurisdictions; $7-13M charges; $20-25M annualized savings; further cuts evaluated through end 2027. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001490281/000149028126000013/grpn-20260521.htm ; https://hoodline.com/2026/05/groupon-s-chicago-hq-rocked-as-400-jobs-get-cut-in-ai-shakeup/
- Reported functions hit: HR, customer service, software engineering, operations (PeopleMatters link above; Bloomberg Law https://news.bloomberglaw.com/bankruptcy-law/groupon-to-cut-up-to-400-jobs-coo-jiri-ponrt-to-resign). COO Ponrt left July 10, 2026.
- Czech relocation: NOT found that roles were explicitly "moved to Czechia." Czech-led management (Pale Fire Capital / Senkypl) and earlier plan to cut customer service from ~1,500 to 100-200 (Czech press, undated snippet: https://cc.cz/pale-fire-capital-vysilaji-na-zachranu-grouponu-ex-sefku-slevomatu-havlickova-nastavi-jeho-premenu/). Treat as inference-only.
- 10-K: works councils/trade unions require consultation before restructuring (international ops).
